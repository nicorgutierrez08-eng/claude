The Core Mechanic

You Never Give a Score.
You Just Pick.

Every other movie app asks "how many stars?" Movi asks something harder and more honest: which did you like more?

Head-to-head comparisons force you to be specific. After ~6 picks, a score emerges — not because you typed it, but because your own choices revealed it. The result is a ranked list that actually reflects your taste, not a pile of 4-star ratings that mean nothing.

This is what makes Movi nothing like IMDb, Letterboxd, or Rotten Tomatoes. Those apps ask what score you give something. Movi never asks that.

6 Steps

How Ranking Works

Takes 90 seconds. Produces a ranking that actually means something.

1

Search & Add

Search any film. Movi pulls in full metadata — director, cast, runtime, genre, poster — from TMDB's database of 1M+ titles.

2

Initial Gut Reaction

Pick your honest response: Loved it, It was fine, or Didn't like it. This anchors your score before comparisons begin.

3

Add Context (Optional)

Tag the occasion (First Watch, Rewatch, In Theaters), who you watched with, a 280-char micro-review, and a photo.

4

Head-to-Head Comparisons THE MAGIC

Two films. You pick one. Repeat ~6 times. Each pick teaches the algorithm exactly what your taste is made of.

5

Score Revealed

A precise score (e.g. 8.4) drops in. Not because you typed it — because your comparisons earned it.

6

Your List Updates

Every film shifts to its correct position. Your entire personal canon reorders itself with every new entry.
